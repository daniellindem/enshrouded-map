Plan your next base.

  • Explore the world

    Drag the map to pan. Scroll, pinch, or use + / − to zoom. Search for a settlement or ancient spire.

  • Place your flame

    Click “Place a flame altar” or press A, then choose a spot. Drag a flame to move it. Arrow keys move the selected altar by 1 metre, or 10 with Shift.

  • See room to grow

    Select an altar and change its level. Compare all levels shows the four footprints together. Each altar keeps its own level.

  • Keep your plans

    Changes save in this browser. Share plan copies a link containing all your altars and your map scale.
